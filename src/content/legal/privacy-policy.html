1. Who We Are and Scope

CIRCLEON PTE. LTD. (UEN 202627473C) (“CircleOn”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the CircleOn AI sales platform at https://www.circleon.ai and related applications, APIs, and websites (the “Services”). The Services include Lead Generation, an AI Voice Agent, and a Follow Up engine. At launch, outreach is by email (from mailboxes customers connect) and by voice call (through telephony accounts and phone numbers that customers own); SMS and WhatsApp are coming soon.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal data under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (“PDPA”) in two capacities:

  1. As an organisation (a controller, where that term applies): for our own website, sales, marketing, billing, support, security, and account administration when you deal with CircleOn directly.
  2. As a data intermediary only (a processor, where that term applies): when we process personal data on behalf of our customers, such as their prospects’ contact details, call recordings, transcripts, and message content (“Customer Content”), on their instructions and under our Terms of Service and any Data Processing Addendum (“DPA”), which is available on request at info@circleon.ai. For this data, the customer decides what personal data is processed, whom to contact, what to say, and why. CircleOn processes it only to provide the Services on the customer’s instructions, does not use it for purposes of its own (other than de-identified or aggregated data as described in Section 5), and does not sell it or make it available to other customers.

If you are an individual who spoke with or received a message from a CircleOn customer, that customer is the organisation responsible for how it uses your data, including for having a lawful basis and any consent needed to contact you. The customer’s own privacy notice governs its use of your data; this Policy explains only how CircleOn handles that data as the customer’s data intermediary. Please contact the customer first; we will assist it as its data intermediary.

A short summary of this Policy is set out in our PDPA Notice (https://www.circleon.ai/pdpa). Our approach to the Do Not Call Registry is set out in our DNC Policy (https://www.circleon.ai/dnc-policy). Use of the Services is governed by our Terms of Service (https://www.circleon.ai/terms).

Much B2B contact data (such as a person’s name, job title, business telephone number, business address, and business email address) is “business contact information” under the PDPA. Where it is provided and used solely for business purposes, most of the PDPA’s data protection obligations do not apply to it; we nevertheless protect all contact data handled on the Services in line with this Policy. The PDPA’s Do Not Call Provisions continue to apply to specified messages sent to Singapore telephone numbers (see Section 13).

If you sign in with Google or connect a Gmail / Google Workspace account, Section 15 (Google API Services) explains what Google user data we access and how we use, store, and share it.

The Services are intended for business use by persons aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

5. AI Processing and Model Improvement

CircleOn uses artificial intelligence, including AI service providers, to power voice agents, scoring, personalisation, and related features.

  • When we act as a data intermediary, AI processing is performed on the customer’s instructions to deliver the feature it enabled.
  • We do not sell customer call recordings or prospect lists. Unless a customer’s written agreement with us or a DPA states otherwise, we do not use identifiable Customer Content to train or fine-tune any foundation model, whether owned by us or by a third party, and we do not permit our AI service providers to do so. Personal data (for example a lead’s name, job title, and company, or the content of a call) is sent to AI service providers only to the extent needed to deliver the requested feature.
  • We may use de-identified or aggregated operational data to improve reliability, safety, and product quality. Enterprise customers may negotiate stricter limits in a DPA.

AI outputs can be inaccurate. Customers should review outputs before relying on them.

8. Protection and Data Breaches

We make reasonable security arrangements to protect personal data against unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, copying, modification, disposal, or similar risks, and against the loss of any storage medium or device on which it is stored. Measures may include encryption in transit and at rest, role-based and least-privilege access controls, secure storage of credentials and access tokens for connected mailboxes and telephony accounts, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, logging, staff and contractor confidentiality obligations, and due diligence on service providers.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please use strong passwords and protect your account.

8.1 Data breach notification

If we become aware of a data breach, we will act promptly to contain it and assess whether it is notifiable under the PDPA.

  • Where we are the responsible organisation: if the breach is notifiable, we will notify the PDPC as soon as practicable and no later than 3 calendar days after we assess that it is notifiable, and notify affected individuals where the breach is likely to result in significant harm to them, unless an exception applies.
  • Where we act as a data intermediary: we will notify the affected customer without undue delay after we have credible grounds to believe the breach has occurred, provide information reasonably available to us, and cooperate reasonably so the customer can assess notifiability and meet its own obligations to the PDPC (or other regulator) and affected individuals.

Please report suspected incidents to info@circleon.ai immediately.

13. Do Not Call Registry and Opt-Outs (Singapore)

CircleOn’s screening safeguard. As an additional safeguard, the Services screen Singapore telephone numbers (8-digit numbers starting with 3, 6, 8, or 9) against the Singapore DNC Registry before a voice call is placed or a text message is sent through the Services. Numbers that are listed on the relevant register, have not been checked, or whose results are older than 21 days are not called or messaged. When a person opts out through the Services, the opt-out is recorded and their number or address is suppressed for that customer. Details are in our DNC Policy (https://www.circleon.ai/dnc-policy).

Customer responsibility. Customers remain the sender of their calls and messages and remain solely responsible for compliance with the PDPA Do Not Call Provisions and the Spam Control Act 2007 (see Section 7 of our Terms of Service), including:

  • deciding whether consent or an exemption (for example, an ongoing relationship) applies, and keeping records of it;
  • keeping their contact lists accurate;
  • not concealing or withholding calling line identity, and including the sender’s name or identity and contact information in each message as required;
  • not carrying out dictionary attacks or address-harvesting; and
  • honouring withdrawals of consent and unsubscribe requests received through any channel (within 10 business days where the Spam Control Act 2007 applies).

CircleOn’s screening is not legal advice and is not a guarantee of compliance. SMS and WhatsApp are not yet available; when they launch, the same screening and obligations will apply to them.

CircleOn’s own telemarketing (if any) to Singapore numbers will comply with the PDPA Do Not Call Provisions.

This Section applies when you sign in with Google or connect a Gmail / Google Workspace account to CircleOn. It supplements the rest of this Policy; if there is a conflict regarding Google user data, this Section prevails.

We request only the permissions needed for the features you choose to use. Depending on those features, this may include:

We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features of CircleOn that you request, specifically to:

  • authenticate you and secure your account;
  • send outreach and follow-up emails from your mailbox on your instructions;
  • detect replies, bounces, and unsubscribe requests so that sequences can stop and opt-outs are honoured; and
  • display relevant conversations in CircleOn and, at your request, generate AI-assisted suggested replies or summaries for you.

We do not:

  • sell Google user data;
  • use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, including retargeting, personalised, or interest-based advertising;
  • use or transfer Google user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes;
  • use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalised or non-personalised AI or machine-learning models; or
  • transfer Google user data to others, except as necessary to provide or improve the user-facing features you use (for example, to our cloud hosting and AI service providers acting on our instructions and bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations), for security purposes (such as investigating abuse), to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets with your explicit prior consent.

15.3 Human access

We do not allow humans to read your Google user data unless: (a) we have your affirmative agreement to view specific messages (for example, when you ask our support team for help); (b) it is necessary for security purposes, such as investigating abuse or a security incident; (c) it is necessary to comply with applicable law; or (d) the data has been aggregated and anonymised and is used for internal operations in accordance with applicable law.

15.4 Storage and protection

Google user data is stored with our third-party cloud hosting and storage providers, which may store or process it outside Singapore (see Section 7). We protect it using the measures described in Section 8, including encryption in transit and at rest, secure storage of OAuth access and refresh tokens, and access restricted to personnel and systems that need it to operate the Services.
