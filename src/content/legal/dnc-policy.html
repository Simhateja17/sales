Do Not Call (DNC) Policy

CIRCLEON PTE. LTD. (UEN 202627473C)
Last updated: 6 October 2026

1. Purpose

This policy explains how the CircleOn platform (the “Services”), operated by CIRCLEON PTE. LTD. (UEN 202627473C) (“CircleOn”, “we”, or “us”), helps customers respect Singapore’s Do Not Call (DNC) Registry and opt-out requests. It forms part of, and should be read with, our Terms of Service (https://www.circleon.ai/terms).

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • Singapore telephone numbers, meaning 8-digit numbers starting with 3, 6, 8, or 9 (with or without the +65 country code); and
  • voice calls placed by the AI Voice Agent through a customer’s own telephony account, and SMS and WhatsApp messages once those channels are available.

Email outreach is not covered by the DNC Registry. It is covered by the Spam Control Act 2007 (see Section 6).

3. How screening works

As an additional safeguard, before a voice call is placed or a text message is sent through the Services:

  • each Singapore number is checked against the relevant register of the DNC Registry (the voice call register or the text message register);
  • numbers that are listed on the relevant register are not called or messaged;
  • numbers that have not been checked, or whose results are older than 21 days, are not called or messaged until a fresh result is obtained;
  • numbers that are not valid Singapore numbers in the format above are not called or messaged; and
  • numbers on the customer’s opt-out (suppression) list are not called or messaged.

Screening status is checked when a campaign is launched and again immediately before each call or message is sent. If the check cannot be completed, the call or message is not sent. Before launching a voice campaign, the customer must confirm that the contacts may be contacted for that campaign.

4. Customer responsibilities

CircleOn provides the screening as a technology safeguard only. The customer is the sender of every call and message and remains solely responsible for compliance with Part 9 of the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (the Do Not Call Provisions) and other applicable laws, including:

  • deciding whether clear and unambiguous consent or an exemption (for example, an ongoing relationship) applies, and keeping records of that consent or exemption;
  • keeping contact lists accurate and up to date;
  • including the sender’s name or identity and contact information in each message as required;
  • not concealing or withholding calling line identity;
  • not carrying out dictionary attacks or address-harvesting; and
  • honouring opt-out requests received through any channel, including outside the Services.

5. Opt-outs

If a person says on a call that they do not want to be contacted, or replies “STOP” to a text message once text channels are available, the Services add that number to the customer’s suppression list, and it will not be called or messaged again for that customer. Customers can also add numbers to their suppression list. Customers must give effect to opt-outs they receive in any other way.

6. Email outreach

For commercial emails, customers must comply with the Spam Control Act 2007, including, where it applies, labelling the subject line with “<ADV>”, using accurate sender information, including a working unsubscribe facility, and giving effect to unsubscribe requests within 10 business days.

7. Records

The Services keep records of DNC screening results, call and message decisions, opt-outs, and campaign launch confirmations. These records help customers demonstrate compliance. They are kept as described in our Privacy Policy (https://www.circleon.ai/privacy). Customers remain responsible for keeping their own consent and exemption records.

8. No guarantee

DNC screening and suppression are aids only. They are not legal advice and are not a guarantee of compliance. CircleOn does not assess consent or exemptions. CircleOn’s responsibility for the Services, including screening, is limited as set out in our Terms of Service (https://www.circleon.ai/terms).
