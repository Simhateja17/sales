PDPA Notice (Singapore)

CIRCLEON PTE. LTD. (UEN 202627473C)
Last updated: 6 October 2026

This notice summarises how CircleOn handles personal data under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (“PDPA”). It supplements our Privacy Policy (https://www.circleon.ai/privacy), which gives full details. If this notice and the Privacy Policy differ, the Privacy Policy prevails.

1. Who we are

CircleOn is operated by CIRCLEON PTE. LTD. (UEN 202627473C), a Singapore company (“CircleOn”, “we”, or “us”). CircleOn provides an AI sales platform with Lead Generation, an AI Voice Agent, and a Follow Up engine (the “Services”). At launch, outreach is by email and by voice call through telephony accounts that our customers own; SMS and WhatsApp are coming soon.

2. Our role

  • Organisation: we are the organisation responsible for personal data about our own customers, users, website visitors, and enquirers, such as account, billing, support, and website data.
  • Data intermediary: for personal data that our customers upload to or generate through the Services (such as their prospects’ contact details, emails, call recordings, and transcripts), we act only as the customer’s data intermediary and process it on the customer’s instructions. The customer is the organisation responsible for that data, including for having consent or another lawful basis to contact you. If a CircleOn customer contacted you, please contact that customer first; we will assist it.

3. Personal data we collect

  • From customers and users: name, work email, phone number, company, job title, billing details, account activity, and communications with us.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser information, usage data, cookies, and logs.
  • On behalf of customers: business contact information of prospects (name, role, company, business email, and business phone number) from third-party lead data providers or customer uploads, together with emails, replies, call recordings, transcripts, DNC Registry screening results, and opt-out records.

4. Purposes

We collect, use, and disclose personal data to provide and support the Services; operate customer-connected integrations; screen Singapore numbers against the DNC Registry and honour opt-outs; process billing; keep the Services secure and prevent abuse; improve the Services using de-identified or aggregated data; communicate with you; and comply with the law.

5. Consent and withdrawal

By using the Services or giving us your personal data, you consent to the purposes above, as notified to you. You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing info@circleon.ai or, for marketing emails, by using the unsubscribe link. We will tell you about the likely consequences of withdrawal and give effect to it within a reasonable time. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out. To stop contact from a CircleOn customer, tell that customer directly, for example by saying so on the call; the Services record such opt-outs for that customer.

6. Access and correction

You may ask for access to the personal data we hold about you, and for information on how it has been used or disclosed in the past year, and you may ask us to correct an error or omission in it. Email info@circleon.ai with the subject “PDPA Access/Correction Request”. We may need to verify your identity. We will respond within 30 days or, if we cannot, tell you within 30 days when we will be able to respond. We may charge a reasonable fee for access requests, and we will tell you the fee in advance.

7. Accuracy

We make reasonable efforts to ensure personal data we collect is accurate and complete. Please keep your account details up to date. Customers are responsible for the accuracy of the data they upload.

8. Protection

We make reasonable security arrangements to protect personal data, including access controls, encryption in transit, secure storage of credentials, and confidentiality obligations for our staff and service providers.

9. Retention

We keep only the personal data needed to run the Services, and for customer data, only on the customer’s instructions. We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. When an account ends, or on request, we delete or return customer data within a reasonable period, except where the law requires us to keep it (for example, billing records), where we need it to establish or defend legal claims or to demonstrate compliance (for example, DNC screening and opt-out records), and for copies in routine backups until they are overwritten. Details are in Section 10 of our Privacy Policy.

10. Third-party service providers

We use the following categories of third-party service providers, under contract, to host, store, and process data:

  • cloud hosting and storage providers;
  • AI service providers;
  • lead data providers;
  • payment processors;
  • telephony providers, through accounts that our customers own; and
  • email and mailbox providers that customers connect.

We do not sell personal data.

11. Transfers outside Singapore

Our service providers may store or process personal data outside Singapore. Before any transfer, we take steps under the PDPA Transfer Limitation Obligation, such as contractual safeguards and reliance on recognised provider certifications where available, to ensure the data receives protection comparable to that under the PDPA.

12. Data breach notification

If a data breach occurs, we will act promptly to contain and assess it. If we assess that a breach is notifiable, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commission (“PDPC”) no later than 3 calendar days after that assessment, and notify affected individuals where required. Where the breach affects data we process for a customer, we will notify that customer without undue delay so it can meet its own obligations.

13. Do Not Call Registry

As an additional safeguard, before a voice call is placed or a text message is sent through the Services, the Services screen Singapore telephone numbers (8-digit numbers starting with 3, 6, 8, or 9) against the DNC Registry. Numbers that are listed on the relevant register, have not been checked, or whose results are older than 21 days are not called or messaged, and opt-outs made through the Services are added to the customer’s suppression list. This screening is not legal advice and is not a guarantee of compliance. Our customers are the senders of their calls and messages and remain solely responsible for complying with the PDPA Do Not Call Provisions. See our DNC Policy (https://www.circleon.ai/dnc-policy).
